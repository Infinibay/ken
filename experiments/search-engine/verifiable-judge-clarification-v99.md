# General rubric clarification before principal review

Calibration pass/fail agreement was60/60 with0/40false accepts. Some ordinal R/S
ratings differ across curator and consumer; do not interpret that as calibrated
ordinal accuracy. All original calibration judgments and labels remain intact.
No case-specific expected rating, identity, span or hidden-source fact is given
as feedback. These clarifications preserve the original criterion and budgets.

- Relevance and sufficiency are independent. A complete but clearly wrong piece
  can beR0/S2 when visible evidence lets the consumer conclusively reject an
  essential request. S2 is not a length/completeness bonus. If decisive facts
  remain outside the card, useS0/1 as appropriate instead.
- R0 needs visible evidence of an unrelated/wrong responsibility. R1 fits related
  evidence that does not establish the literal requested responsibility. A sparse
  card is not automatically irrelevant. R2 can reflect the correct declared
  responsibility even when implementation details still need more evidence.
- Metadata establishes the purported canonical identity/type/path, not behavior.
  A declared contract can answer a contract/responsibility question; code is
  required when the literal request asks implementation decisions or conditions.
  A suggestive helper name does not prove hidden behavior. Caller/helper evidence
  needs a visible relationship to the requested responsibility.
- Judge the literal whole query. Public facets organize essentials but do not
  authorize ignoring other essential conditions stated in that query. Missing
  evidence stays unobservable; an absence claim needs sufficient relevant scope.
- A pass still requiresR2/S2, every public facet supported by literal current-card
  citations, faithful source and the correct requested piece. Never require the
  curator's preferred fragment or infer facts from another card/remembered source.

Main review stays cross-curator, anonymous and current-card-only. No other-stratum
Gold, source browsing, engines, scores or mapping. Preserve all judgments before
any principalGold. Extra shadow selections receive the same review after scalar
calibration; no principalGold until that review also seals. Abstention stays a
failure in the original200 denominator.
