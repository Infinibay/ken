# Authentic evidence versus renderer selection

The v98 comparison renderer, card selection, ranking, source validator, tests,
candidate pools and acceptance criteria remain frozen. This amendment changes
only validation of separately curated calibration cards, before their public
contents or labels are opened by the implementer.

Source authenticity establishes the primary canonical identity and the literal
bytes, physical ranges, hash and actual owner of EVERY excerpt, including a
properly attributed helper in another owner/file. It enforces the same 64-line,
four-excerpt and 12000-character budgets. It does not establish relevance.

The blind consumer must see a sufficient relationship between the primary unit,
the request and the supporting evidence. An authentic unrelated helper cannot
prove the primary implements the behavior. A wrapper is a valid alternative
only when the literal request concerns that wrapper/entry responsibility and
the visible caller/helper evidence establishes all requested facets with the
same preconditions. Delegating to the implementation does not make a wrapper
the implementation of an algorithm. A type stub proves only its stated contract.

The v98 renderer currently restricts symbol cards to their owner and class cards
to their owner/members. A correct identity can therefore fail evidence coverage
when it needs a helper; this remains a measured failure. Calibration witnesses
are not restricted to shortcomings of this renderer. No Gold-selected support
is injected into live answers, and no preexisting failure becomes a success
merely by authenticating it. R2/S2 and supported literal citations for every
public facet remain necessary for a verifiable success.

Calibration can be source-authenticated and anonymized before neural scoring,
so model-free blind review can proceed during serial CPU measurements. Reviewers
see no labels, scores, engines or variants. The sixty calibration judgments must
close before feedback. Calibration scalar scoring stays separate from measured
full-pipeline timings. No principal Gold may open before final blind judgments.
